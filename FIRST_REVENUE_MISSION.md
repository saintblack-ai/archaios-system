# FIRST REVENUE MISSION

Date: 2026-06-12
Founder-led seller: Saint Black
Product: AI Assassins / ARCHAIOS
Primary objective: obtain revenue before scaling

## Mission Principle

Do not try to "launch to everyone." The first mission is to personally sell a small number of paid pilots, learn why people buy, then turn that proof into a scalable funnel.

Revenue sequence:

1. First customer: close one Pro pilot at `$49/month` or one Elite pilot at `$99/month`.
2. Five customers: prove repeatability across one clear audience segment.
3. Ten customers: prove early traction, onboarding, retention, and upgrade potential.

Best first offer:

- Pro: `$49/month` full dashboard for daily intelligence, alerts, and execution workflows.
- Elite: `$99/month` priority intelligence with deeper reports and high-urgency signal review.
- Founder-led bonus: first 10 customers get a personal onboarding call and one custom intelligence brief.

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Primary ICP

Independent operators who make decisions from public information but do not have time to scan global events, markets, risk signals, and opportunity windows every day.

Best-fit profiles:

- Solo founders and early-stage startup operators.
- Independent investors, traders, crypto operators, and market watchers.
- Consultants, strategists, coaches, and agency owners.
- Content creators who sell premium insight, newsletters, courses, or communities.
- Local business owners who want sharper market/risk awareness without hiring an analyst.

### Buying Triggers

- "I am overloaded by information."
- "I need a daily briefing before I make moves."
- "I want sharper signals for content, investments, business, or strategy."
- "I want to look more informed in front of clients/community."
- "I already pay for tools but still have to connect the dots myself."

### Qualification Checklist

Good prospect:

- Posts or talks about AI, business, investing, markets, geopolitics, strategy, content, or entrepreneurship.
- Already buys tools, newsletters, SaaS, or paid communities.
- Has visible business activity or audience.
- Can benefit from one daily signal that saves time, creates content, or improves decisions.

Poor prospect:

- Wants everything free.
- Has no clear use for daily intelligence.
- Asks for custom automation before buying.
- Needs enterprise compliance before the product has traction.

## Value Proposition

Short version:

ARCHAIOS turns scattered global signals into a daily operator briefing so founders, creators, and strategists can move faster without drowning in noise.

Pro value:

For `$49/month`, Pro gives the full dashboard: daily intelligence, alerts, saved context, and execution workflows.

Elite value:

For `$99/month`, Elite adds priority intelligence: deeper signal review, high-urgency context, and faster decision support.

Founder-led pilot value:

The first customers are not just buying software. They are getting direct founder onboarding from Saint Black, early influence on the product, and a custom intelligence sample tailored to their world.

# BUSINESS BANKING REQUIREMENTS

Audit date: 2026-06-10
Purpose: Prepare ARCHAIOS / AI Assassins for clean business banking.

## Banking Objective

Open a business checking account under the LLC before enabling live Stripe billing. This separates business funds from personal funds and gives Stripe a valid payout destination.

The SBA says common bank account requirements include EIN, business formation documents, ownership agreements, and business license where applicable.

Source: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/open-business-bank-account

## Required Banking Packet

| Document/Data | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Initial deposit source | Needed | Personal contribution or business funds |

## Bank Selection Criteria

| Criterion | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly fees | Prefer low or waived fees |
| ACH support | Required for vendor/subscription operations |
| Debit card | Required for software subscriptions |
| Accounting export | Required for reconciliation |
| Stripe compatibility | Required for payouts |
| Support responsiveness | Important during verification |
| Business credit path | Useful after revenue starts |

## Recommended Accounts

1. Business checking: required.
2. Business savings: optional but useful for tax reserve.
3. Business credit card: optional after bank approval.
4. Merchant/payment account: Stripe after bank account is active.

| Marketing/ads | Paid acquisition |
| Professional services | Legal, tax, accounting |
| Owner contributions | Founder funding |
| Tax reserve | Income/sales/franchise tax reserve |

## Banking Controls

- No personal revenue collection once the LLC bank account exists.
- No live Stripe payouts to personal account after business bank is active.
- Keep monthly reconciliation schedule.
- Export Stripe monthly reports.
- Keep receipts for all software and infrastructure expenses.
- Separate tax reserve after first revenue.

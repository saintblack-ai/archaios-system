## IP Categories

The USPTO distinguishes:

- Trademark: words, phrases, designs, or combinations that identify source of goods/services.
- Patent: technical inventions and processes.
- Copyright: original works fixed in tangible form, including software code, writing, music, images, and other creative works.

Source: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/trademark-patent-copyright

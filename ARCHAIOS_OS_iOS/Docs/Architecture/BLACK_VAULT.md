# Black Vault

## Purpose

Black Vault is the local archive and research memory for ARCHAIOS OS. It stores documents, research notes, journal material, prompt library records, and knowledge collections without live Notion, Supabase, or cloud calls.

## Main Sources

- `BlackVaultView`
- `BlackVaultViewModel`
- `VaultEntry`
- `ResearchNote`
- `JournalEntry`
- `KnowledgeNode`
- `KnowledgeCollectionRecord`

## Current Features

- categories
- search
- pinned-style document sections
- recent and favorite document concepts
- reading progress and metadata concepts
- local AI search surfaces from later sprints
- Sprint 17 Knowledge Engine categories:
  - Books
  - Research
  - Prompt Library
  - Architecture
  - Black Vault
  - Music
  - Military
  - Philosophy
  - Ideas

## Relationship Role

Black Vault now participates in the Living Intelligence relationship layer. Vault records can be conceptually related to:

- missions
- conversations
- journal entries
- research notes
- music ideas
- prompt templates
- knowledge nodes
- executive decisions

## Integration Policy

Black Vault is local-only in the iOS app. Notion and cloud archive integration remain future bridge concepts only.

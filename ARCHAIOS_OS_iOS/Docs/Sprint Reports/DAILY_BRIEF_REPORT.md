# DAILY_BRIEF_REPORT

## Executive Daily Brief Sections

- Today's Mission
- Top Priorities
- Recent Decisions
- Research Progress
- Sprint Progress
- Open Tasks
- Suggested Next Action
- Mission Health
- Focus Score

## Storage

Daily briefs are generated locally and stored in SwiftData through `DailyBriefRecord`.

## Determinism

The brief is composed from local mission queue, decision log, research, sprint, and readiness signals. No AI service or network call is used.

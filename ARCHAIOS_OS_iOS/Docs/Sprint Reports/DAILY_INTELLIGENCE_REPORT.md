# DAILY_INTELLIGENCE_REPORT

## Daily Intelligence Cycle

1. Morning Brief: set today's focus from commander memory, active mission, newest research, and local system health.
2. Mission Execution: move the active mission forward and preserve the next commander decision.
3. Research Collection: collect local research and link it to missions, books, technology, engineering, music, or architecture.
4. Journal Reflection: capture founder reflection, energy, mood, and context.
5. Evening Debrief: close the loop with outcomes, blockers, resume point, and tomorrow's first action.

## Local Suggestions

- Continue Mission.
- Resume Research.
- Expand Black Vault.
- Finish Sprint.
- Review Engineering.
- Update Documentation.
- Continue Album Notes.
- Review Journal.

All suggestions are generated from local SwiftData state and deterministic rules.

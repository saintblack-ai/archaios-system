# MISSION_GRAPH_REPORT

## Graph Sources

- Missions and saved missions.
- Research notes.
- Founder journal and journal entries.
- Music project notes.
- Black Vault entries.
- Prompt templates.
- Knowledge collections.
- Executive decisions.
- Work sessions.
- Sprint timeline events.

## Relationship Rules

- Saved relationships are displayed directly from local `IntelligenceRelationshipRecord` data.
- Inferred relationships are generated by matching meaningful local keywords across node titles and details.
- Selecting any mission reveals related research, journal, sprint, music, engineering, commander decisions, notes, and documents where local data overlaps.

## Visual Layer

The Living Intelligence screen renders graph nodes as selectable Founder Edition tiles with type icons, labels, titles, and relationship weight.

## Operating Agreement Requirements

The operating agreement should cover:

- Company name and formation state.
- Business purpose.
- Member capital contributions.
- Ownership percentages.
- Profit/loss allocation.
- Management authority.
- Banking authority.
- Tax classification.
- Intellectual property ownership by the LLC.
- Member duties and compensation.
- Admission/removal of members.
- Transfer restrictions.
- Dissolution rules.
- Records and accounting requirements.

## Recommended Formation Sequence

1. Pick formation state and legal name.
2. Search state business name database.
3. Search USPTO for ARCHAIOS and AI Assassins.
4. Choose registered agent and business address.
5. Prepare operating agreement.
6. File LLC with state.
7. Save stamped/accepted formation document.
8. Apply for EIN with IRS.
9. Open business bank account.

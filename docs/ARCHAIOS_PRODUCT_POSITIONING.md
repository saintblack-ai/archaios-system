# ARCHAIOS Product Positioning

ARCHAIOS is an AI-assisted intelligence and research operating system that transforms open, authorized data into sourced situation reports, research missions, semantic memory, system-health awareness, and interactive geographic analysis.

ARCHAIOS is for operators who need disciplined awareness, source memory, research continuity, and decision support without confusing model interpretation with verified fact.

It is not marketed as a government, military, intelligence-agency, or classified system.

# ARCHAIOS Ethics And Source Policy

ARCHAIOS only uses open, official, public, user-owned, or explicitly authorized sources.

## Source Rules

- Preserve source name and URL for every intelligence event.
- Label sample, cached, stale, unavailable, and live data.
- Do not fabricate threat intelligence, military claims, coordinates, or source confirmations.
- Treat external feed text as untrusted input.
- Separate facts from Commander or model interpretation.
- Do not present model-generated conclusions as verified facts.

## Public Language

Product language must remain credible for investors, employers, researchers, veterans, creators, and customers. Creative command identity can shape tone, but factual claims require evidence.

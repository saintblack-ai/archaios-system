# ARCHAIOS Founder Charter

Founder: Quandrix Lee Blackburn  
Creative identity: Saint Black

ARCHAIOS is an AI-assisted intelligence and research operating system designed to transform open and authorized data into sourced situation reports, research missions, semantic memory, system-health awareness, and interactive geographic analysis.

## Foundation

ARCHAIOS preserves the founder's technical, creative, communications, and disciplined research work in a durable operating system. The Saint Black identity may inform visual language and creative direction, but public intelligence products must remain professional, sourced, and credible.

## Commitments

- Maintain source integrity.
- Separate verified facts from interpretation.
- Preserve work for future generations.
- Protect user data and private research.
- Treat public feeds as untrusted until validated.
- Keep service and communications background framed as professional experience, not unsupported government or classified affiliation.

## Boundaries

ARCHAIOS may contain artistic mythology, founder narrative, and creative command language in private or branded archive contexts. Those elements must not be mixed into factual intelligence reports, system-health reports, or investor/customer-facing claims unless clearly labeled as brand language.
